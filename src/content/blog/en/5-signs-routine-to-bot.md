## 1. You answer the same questions every day

"How much is it?", "Any free slot tomorrow?", "Where are you located?". If most messages are the same 10–15 questions, they don't need a person. A bot answers just as politely, in seconds, without pulling the stylist or receptionist away from the client in the chair.

## 2. Evening and weekend requests wait until morning

A client writes at 10 pm and gets a reply at 10 am. By then they've booked with whoever answered first. A bot works 24/7 and offers free time right away, so an evening request becomes a booking, not a lost client.

## 3. Bookings live in someone's head, a notebook or several chats

When bookings are scattered across Instagram, Viber, calls and a notebook, sooner or later there's a double booking or a forgotten client. A bot puts everyone into one sheet or calendar, so you always see the full picture of the day.

## 4. Clients don't show up because they forgot

Reminding everyone by hand means dozens of messages a week. Usually it happens "when there's a minute", and on busy days not at all. A bot sends a reminder a day before with "I'll be there" and "Reschedule" buttons, and the freed slot can go straight to another client.

## 5. You don't know what you earned today until you count it by hand

If finding out the number of bookings, cancellations and the day's total means scrolling through chats, that's one more routine. A bot can send the owner a short report every evening: how many bookings, who didn't show, how much money.

## How to tell if it pays off

A simple rule: **running the bot should cost less than one hour of the work it frees up**. Example: if a receptionist spends 1.5 hours a day on repeated answers and reminders, that's about 45 hours a month. Running a bot costs a few dollars a month plus a one-off setup. The difference is obvious.

## What stays with people

A bot doesn't replace the stylist or handle tricky situations. It takes the repetitive work: answers, bookings, reminders, reports. The receptionist and the owner focus on clients, service quality and growth.
